OpenRouter

No key ships with this app — "Add with AI" needs your own free OpenRouter key to talk to an AI model. To get one:

  1. Open openrouter.ai/keys and sign in (or create a free account).
  2. Click Create key, name it anything, and copy the key it gives you.
  3. Paste it into the field above.

The key is saved only in this browser's local storage and sent directly from your device to OpenRouter — it never touches any server of ours. It's included in DB export/import along with your model and goals, so keep exported files private. Pick any OpenRouter model slug that supports image input for photo analysis.

Profile

Used by Automatic mode's static estimate (Mifflin-St Jeor) until you have enough logged data for a personalized target. Body weight edits the day currently selected on the Log tab.

Daily goals

Leave any field blank to hide it from the Log tab's progress bars. Kcal and protein/carbs/fat stay in sync (4 / 4 / 9 kcal per gram): editing a macro recalculates kcal, and editing kcal rescales the macros to match.

Turn Automatic on to let Fitny recalculate your kcal goal from your goal type, your logged intake, and your weight trend over the last two weeks. While it's on, manual goal edits last until the next adjustment.

Data

Export everything — log, weight, settings, API key and goals — as a JSON file, or import one on another device. Keep exported files private since they contain your API key.
