OpenRouter

No key ships with this app — get one at openrouter.ai/keys and paste it above. It's saved in this browser's local storage and sent directly from your device to OpenRouter. It's included in DB export/import along with your model and goals, so keep exported files private. Pick any OpenRouter model slug that supports image input for photo analysis.

Daily goals

Leave any field blank to hide it from the Log tab's progress bars. Kcal and protein/carbs/fat stay in sync (4 / 4 / 9 kcal per gram): editing a macro recalculates kcal, and editing kcal rescales the macros to match.

Data
